01 / MY STORY THE PERSON BEHIND THE WORK

I'm a student, a creator, and a lifelong learner.

My journey is shaped by questions, experiments, challenges, and the excitement of discovering something new.

I'm currently a student in Rwanda with a strong interest in mathematics, physics, chemistry, and technology. I enjoy understanding how things work, exploring new ideas, and finding ways to apply what I learn.

Technology has become one of the ways I express that curiosity. Through coding and digital projects, I get to experiment, solve problems, and turn concepts into practical experiences.

I also believe learning should reach beyond the classroom. Sharing knowledge, collaborating with others, and contributing to my community are important parts of the person I want to become.

Curious by nature. Growing by choice.
